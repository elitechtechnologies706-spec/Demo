---
name: CRVPF story safeguarding
description: Anonymity, consent labeling, and location limits for story content and imagery in the CRVPF demo.
---

For CRVPF story and youth-voice content, do not display personal names, ages, family status, number of children, or precise locations. Use country-level context and the requested consent/anonymity badge on story cards. Replace the specified unapproved hero, story, and resource photos with neutral, no-face illustrations and clearly label them as pending CRVPF approval.

**Why:** the user set this as a safeguarding requirement for the CRVPF demo and requested that child and participant details not be identifiable.

**How to apply:** check homepage, impact, programme, annual-report, portal, and publication story views together; do not rely on a single shared story list to cover every display.
